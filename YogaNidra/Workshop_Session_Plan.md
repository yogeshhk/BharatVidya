# Yoga Nidra workshop: session plan (draft for the author)

A plan for delivering the existing deck (`LaTeX/Main_Seminar_YogaNidra_*`) as a practice-led workshop. The timings are proposals, not measured; the author adjusts them after the first delivery. No new slides are needed. The practice itself follows [YogaNidraDetailedScript.md](YogaNidraDetailedScript.md).

## Settings and length

| Setting | Length | Mix |
|---|---|---|
| Academic or meetup | 90 minutes | Short theory, one full practice, a short teaching segment |
| Social or community group | 60 minutes | Almost no theory, one full practice, questions |
| Half day | 3 hours | Two practices, the teaching segment, the Shrimath material |

## The 90-minute plan

| Minutes | Block | Deck section | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | Welcome and what Yoga Nidra is (nidra against Yoga Nidra) | Intro | Ask how many have done it before |
| 10 to 25 | Theory in plain words: where it sits (pratyahara), what it does | Theory | Keep to the frames that matter for a first-timer; skip the rest |
| 25 to 35 | Set up the room and the body, explain the stages | Instructions | Mats or chairs, blankets, lights low, phones off |
| 35 to 65 | The practice: preparation, initial relaxation, sankalpa, rotation of consciousness, breath, feelings and sensations, visualisation, sankalpa again, externalisation | The script | The author guides; no slides on screen |
| 65 to 75 | Sit up slowly, share in pairs or write one line | | Do not ask people to share the content of the practice |
| 75 to 85 | How to teach it: pacing, voice, what to say and not say | Teaching | For participants who will guide others |
| 85 to 90 | Questions and close | End | Point to the notes and the reading list |

The Shrimath frames (the author's notes from the course) suit the half-day version only.

## What to bring

- Script and a printed copy of the stages; a soft timer; a quiet room.
- A short written safety note read aloud before the practice: it is relaxation, not treatment; anyone may open their eyes or stop at any time; for a medical condition or a history of distress, ask a professional first (the exact wording is for the author to check against the course notes).
- Mats or chairs, blankets, a clock that is not a phone.
