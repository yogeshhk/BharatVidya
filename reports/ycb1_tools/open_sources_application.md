## Application of knowledge: open source gap

## Found in deck

- ycb1_practical_application.tex and ycb1_exam.tex give only the weight: 10 of the 140 practical marks, inside a practical of at most one hour per candidate.
- The exam deck lists candidate-reported topics (planning sessions for a disabled person, diabetes, high blood pressure, back pain, students, women; one candidate line says "application of knowledge-based questions" on meditation techniques). These are candidate reports, not Board text.
- The application deck's skill list (protocol, tailored instruction, assessment, lesson adaptation, communication) is from the guidebook's teacher competence list; the Board does not say this component tests it.

## Found on web

- https://yogacertificationboard.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Guideline-for-YP-Version-0.2.pdf : the Board guideline lists application of knowledge and field experience as the practicum parts with their marks (10 for this level); gives no description or rubric of the component.
- Scribd copy of the Level 1 syllabus (https://www.scribd.com/document/633185818/Syllabus-Level-1-pdf): same mark split (demonstration 80, teaching 40, application 10, field experience 10); no criteria. Unofficial copy.
- Two searches (Board, institutes, candidate pages) found no page that describes what is assessed.

## Still open

- What the examiner actually asks or checks for this 10-mark component: no Board or reputable institute text found.
- Whether it is a separate viva, scenario questions, or judged inside the demonstration and teaching. Needs the Board's practical assessment format or an approved training institute's written description.
- Until then the deck states only that the Board does not describe it, and keeps the case-study preparation as a labelled study suggestion.
