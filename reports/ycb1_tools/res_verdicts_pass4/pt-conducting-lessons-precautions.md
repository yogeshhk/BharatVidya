# Pass 4 verdicts: ycb1-pt-conducting-lessons-precautions (Conducting practical lessons: precautions and contraindications)

Sources used in this file:
- Common Yoga Protocol, Ministry of Ayush (4th revised edition 2019 at mea.gov.in; 2015 copy in docs): Disclaimer and General Guidelines (before, during and after practice).
- Board Guideline for Yoga Professionals, version 0.3 (docs): functions of the Yoga Protocol Instructor; the separate Yoga Therapy category.

## Open items

- **Safety (CORE, not confirmed).** CORRECTED.
  - Old: "ask about health before the class; correct by word and demonstration first; touch only with the student's consent and with care; stop a student who feels pain, dizziness or breathlessness."
  - New: "before the class, ask about illness, chronic disease, pain, heart trouble, pregnancy and menstruation; such a person consults a physician, yoga therapist or yoga expert first; correct by word and demonstration first; keep every student within his or her own capacity, slowly, with no strain or jerk; nobody practises when exhausted, ill, in a hurry or in acute stress."
  - Source: Common Yoga Protocol General Guidelines (not in exhaustion, illness, hurry or acute stress; consult a physician or yoga therapist in chronic disease, pain or cardiac problems; consult yoga experts in pregnancy and menstruation; slowly, own capacity, do not hold tightly or jerk).
  - "Touch only with the student's consent": REMOVE-OR-MARK-UNVERIFIED. Searched the Board guideline, the protocol and the Board booklet: no named Indian source states it. Keep it only as a labelled teaching suggestion, or drop it.
  - "Stop at dizziness or breathlessness": REMOVE-OR-MARK-UNVERIFIED as worded (no named source); the CORRECTED line keeps the sourced rule (own capacity, no strain).
- **Scope of the instructor (CORE, not confirmed).** CORRECTED.
  - Old: "teach the common protocol for prevention and well-being; do not diagnose or promise a cure; refer the person to a doctor or another teacher when the need is beyond your scope."
  - New: "teach the common protocol for the prevention of disease and the promotion of health; the protocol is not a substitute for a physician's advice, and yoga therapy is a separate Board certification; refer the person to a physician, or to a yoga therapist, when the need is beyond the protocol."
  - Source: Board Guideline version 0.3, functions of the Yoga Protocol Instructor and the separate Yoga Therapy category; Common Yoga Protocol Disclaimer (not a substitute for the medical advice of a physician).
