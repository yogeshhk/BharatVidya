## Open items

- **Sarvanga pushti, deck summary (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. Old: "a full-body strength exercise engaging all major muscle groups, done with controlled movement; credited with strength, endurance and balance. Additional caution from the deck: neck pain." New: "Feet as wide apart as possible; fists with the thumbs tucked in, one wrist on the other; bend towards the right ankle; swing the joined arms up, back and round in a circle with the trunk and bring them down towards the left ankle; very slowly." Source: Brahmachari reprint, Sarvanga-pusti. The breath line in the reprint contradicts itself (inhale while raising, then inhale when coming down): REMOVE-OR-MARK-UNVERIFIED for the breath cue. The neck pain caution has no source: mark unverified. The reprint's benefit claims (cures stiff backs, tuberculosis) are cure claims: do not use.
- **Hrid gati, deck summary (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. Old: "a cardio-type practice that mimics running, done at a rhythmic, steady pace with steady breathing; traditionally credited with heart fitness ..." New: "Feet together, elbows bent, fists with the thumbs tucked in. Run about fifty small steps forward on the toes, heels flicking back towards the buttocks, arms thrust forward and back alternately like pistons; then the same distance backward. Breathe sharply and deeply through the nose, which gives an engine-like hiss (hence engine daud, the locomotive exercise)." Source: Brahmachari reprint, Hrd-gati. Its benefit claims are cure claims (obesity): do not use.
