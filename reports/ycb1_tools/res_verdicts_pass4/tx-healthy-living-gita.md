## Items

- **Divine qualities, chapter 16 (deck)**: CORRECTED.
  - Old: "the Gita lists a set of qualities of a person of divine nature, the base of a healthy life in society; the verse list begins with fearlessness and purity of mind."
  - New: "the Gita lists the qualities of a person of divine nature, beginning with fearlessness and purity of mind (16.1-3), and says that they lead to liberation (16.5); the deck reads them as the base of healthy living."

- **Harmony with nature (deck)**: CORRECTED.
  - Old: "live in step with natural rhythms and cycles, favour sattvic living (purity and calm), avoid both indulgence and harsh self-denial, and keep mindfulness in all activity."
  - New: "avoid both excess and too little in eating and sleep (6.16); be temperate in eating and recreation, balanced in work and regulated in sleep (6.17); prefer sattvic food, which supports life, strength, health and contentment (17.8)."
  - Source: Gita 6.16, 6.17 and 17.8. No Gita verse speaks of natural rhythms and cycles: that is dinacharya and ritucharya, so leave it to that block. Drop "mindfulness", which is a modern term not in the text.
