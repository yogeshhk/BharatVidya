## Open items

- **Uttana mandukasana** (tag: source needed). CORRECTED. Old: "No steps are given until a better source is found." New: "Sit in vajrasana; spread the knees wide, big toes together. Raise the right arm, fold it and take it back over the right shoulder, palm below the left shoulder; do the same with the left arm, palm below the right shoulder, so the head rests against the folded arms. Hold for a while; release the left arm, then the right; bring the knees together. Caution: not with severe knee joint pain." Source: Common Yoga Protocol 2016, Uttana Mandukasana (Ministry of AYUSH, MDNIY-edited). The 2016 protocol says the head is held by the elbows, which matches the one-line description already in the notes.
- **Ushtrasana (camel) (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. New: "Kneel; knees and feet about one foot apart; inhale and bend back without jerking the neck; exhale and place the right palm on the right heel and the left palm on the left heel; thighs vertical, head tilted back, weight shared by arms and legs. Caution: not with high blood pressure, heart disease or hernia." Source: Common Yoga Protocol 2016, Ustrasana. The deck caution "back or neck problems" is replaced by the protocol list. The protocol's benefit lines (eyesight and others) are traditional claims: do not repeat.
- **Vajrasana (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED for steps and cautions; CONFIRMED for the after-meal point. New: "From legs stretched out, fold each leg and place the foot under the same-side buttock; big toes overlap, buttocks between the heels; hands on the knees; spine erect. Caution: not with piles; avoid with knee pain or ankle injury." Source: Common Yoga Protocol 2016, Vajrasana. After meals: Satyananda, Asana Pranayama Mudra Bandha, Vajrasana, Duration ("especially directly after meals, for at least 5 minutes"), Board-named book. Replace "(to be checked)" with that source.
- **Sukhasana (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. New: "Sit with the legs out; bend the right leg and place the foot under the left thigh; bend the left leg and place the foot under the right thigh; hands on the knees in chin or jnana mudra; head, neck and back upright without strain; eyes closed." Source: Satyananda, APMB, Sukhasana (printed p. 96 to 97). The cushion advice is teaching advice; APMB's own variation for the very stiff is a cloth tied round the knees and lower back.
- **Paschimottanasana (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. New: "Sit with the legs out, feet together, hands on the knees. Bend forward from the hips on the out-breath, sliding the hands down the legs; hold the big toes, or the heels, ankles or shins if the toes are out of reach; keep the legs straight and bring the trunk down with the arms, not the back; do not strain; return on the in-breath. Beginners up to 5 rounds with a short hold. Caution: not with a slipped disc or sciatica." Source: Satyananda, APMB, Paschimottanasana (printed p. 230 to 231).
- **Uttanapadasana or ardha halasana (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED (they are two postures). New: "Uttanapadasana: supine, hands by the sides; inhale and raise both straight legs to about 30 degrees; hold with normal breathing; exhale and lower; twice. Caution: with high blood pressure, raise one leg at a time without holding the breath. Ardha halasana: supine; raise both straight legs to 30 degrees, then 60, then 90 degrees; hold as long as comfortable; lower slowly without lifting the head. Caution: with lower back pain do not use both legs together; avoid with abdominal injury or hernia." Source: Common Yoga Protocol 2016, Uttana Padasana and Ardha Halasana; Board syllabus v0.2 lists them as separate names.
- **Dandasana** (pass 3: details need the guidebook). CONFIRMED. Board Guideline v0.2 (dandasana start: sit, legs straight, palms beside the buttocks, per pass 3); NIOS Yoga Levels A to C (government); the Common Yoga Protocol 2016 uses dandasana as the starting position (sthiti) for vakrasana.
