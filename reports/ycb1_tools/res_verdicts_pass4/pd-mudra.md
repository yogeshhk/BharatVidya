## Open items

- **Meaning and types (deck, not yet checked): five groups hasta, kaya, mukha, bandha, chakra**. CORRECTED. New: "Mudra means a seal or gesture. The Board syllabus asks only for hasta (hand) mudras. (The Bihar School groups mudras as hasta, mana (head), kaya, bandha and adhara.)" Source: APMB, Five groups of yoga mudras; Board syllabus 4.9. The deck's "mukha" and "chakra" groups are not that grouping.
- **Mukha mudras (deck, not yet checked)**. REMOVE-OR-MARK-UNVERIFIED: outside the Board syllabus (4.9 lists hand mudras only). Remove.
- **Chakra mudras (deck, not yet checked)**. REMOVE-OR-MARK-UNVERIFIED: outside the syllabus, and no standard text was found for the deck's names (the deck's source was a school website). Remove.
- **Chin mudra versus jnana mudra**. NEEDS-HUMAN. Sources differ: APMB gives jnana mudra palms down and chin mudra palms up, with the index finger at the root of the thumb (the thumb and index tips touching is given as a common variation). The Common Yoga Protocol gives jnana mudra as thumb tip to index tip with the palms up. Record the variation in one line; only the Board can say which form the exam expects (the protocol is the Board's practice reference, but it does not describe chin mudra).
- **Chinmaya mudra (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. Old: "the index fingertip touches the thumb tip and the other fingers curl into the palm like a loose fist, palms up." New: "Hold the fingers as for jnana mudra, then fold the three straight fingers so their tips touch or point to the palm; the index finger may touch the root or the tip of the thumb; hands on the knees, palms up or down." Source: Niranjanananda, Prana and Pranayama, Chinmaya mudra. Drop "humility and grounding" (no text).
- **Brahma mudra (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. Old: "both fists are joined and held at the navel." New: "Turn the thumbs in across the palms and fold the fingers over them; place the backs of the hands on the thighs with the knuckles of the two hands touching, little-finger side close to the body, at the level of the pelvic bone." Source: Niranjanananda, Prana and Pranayama, Brahma mudra. The deck's navel level differs from this text; the pelvic level is the sourced one.
- **Adi mudra (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. New: "Fold the thumb into the palm and curl the fingers over it to make a fist; hands on the knees, palms up or down." Source: Niranjanananda, Prana and Pranayama, Aadi mudra.
- **Dhyana mudra (deck, not yet checked): palms up, one on the other, in the lap**. NEEDS-HUMAN for the name. The form (right hand on the left, palms up, resting in the lap) is described by APMB under the name bhairava mudra (left on right is bhairavi). No Board or standard text was found that gives this form under the name dhyana mudra, and the Common Yoga Protocol meditates in jnana mudra. The Board must confirm what it means by dhyana mudra.
- **Nasika mudra**. CONFIRMED. Common Yoga Protocol, nasagra mudra: index and middle fingers folded, ring and little fingers by the left nostril, thumb by the right.
- **"Brahma mudra good for overall health"**. No change: already left out as a health claim in pass 3.
