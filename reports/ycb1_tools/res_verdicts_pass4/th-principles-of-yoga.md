- Tri dosha property lists | CORRECTED | Old: "vata (dry, light, cold, rough, unsteady), pitta (hot, sharp, light, liquid, quick) and kapha (unctuous, cold, heavy, sluggish, soft, steady)". New: "vata (dry, light, cold, rough, subtle, mobile), pitta (slightly oily, sharp, hot, light, foul-smelling, flowing, liquid) and kapha (unctuous, cold, heavy, slow, smooth, slimy, steady)". Source: Ashtanga Hridaya, Sutrasthana ch.1, verses on the doshas (satyori.com Sutrasthana 1.11 with Sanskrit; easyayurveda.com Ashtanga Hrudaya Sutra Sthana ch.1). - Quiz fact: Ajna chakra between the eyebrows | CORRECTED | Old: "the Ajna chakra lies between the eyebrows". Variation line: the Guidebook gives the spinal position; most teaching names the eyebrow point.
