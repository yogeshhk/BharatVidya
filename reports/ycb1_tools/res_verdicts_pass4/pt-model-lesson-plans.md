  - Source: Common Yoga Protocol General Guidelines; Board Chair Yoga syllabus. "More rest" has no named source: drop it or keep it as a labelled suggestion.

  - Source: Common Yoga Protocol 2019 (sequence, institutional-practice note, contraindications kept in mind).
