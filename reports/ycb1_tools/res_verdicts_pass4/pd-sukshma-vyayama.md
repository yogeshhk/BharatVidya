## Open items

- **Bhuja valli and purna bhuja, shoulder (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED (the deck had the two swapped). Old: "arm circles (one arm or both, forward and backward) and shoulder shrugs belong to bhuja valli; shoulder rotations and arm raises belong to purna bhuja." New: "Bhuja valli: feet together; raise one arm sideways above the head, palm open and facing out, and bring it down without touching the head or the thigh; right arm, left arm, then both. Purna bhuja: fists with the thumbs tucked in; swing one arm in a full circle forward, then backward; then the other arm; then both arms." Source: Brahmachari reprint, sections Bhuja-valli and Purna-bhuja sakti vikasaka. The reprint has the breath held during purna bhuja; leave the breath out for Level 1 until the original is read.
- **Kati shakti vikasaka, five forms (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. New: "I: one fist behind the back, the other hand on that wrist; bend back on the in-breath, bend forward on the out-breath towards the knees. II: legs wide apart, hands on the hips; bend back, then forward towards the floor. III: feet together; quick bends back and forward (the reprint says with a jerk). IV: arms stretched out to the sides; bend the trunk to the left and to the right towards the calf, without moving the arms up or down. V: feet two feet apart; swing the trunk and the outstretched arms in a half circle to each side." Source: Brahmachari reprint, Kati sakti vikasaka 1 to 5. The deck's fourth form ("side twist") is a side bend in the text. The cautions line ("back and spine problems, recent abdominal surgery, heart disease") has no source in the reprint: REMOVE or mark unverified. The quick jerk bends of form III raise a safety question that no source answers.
- **Janu shakti vikasaka, knee (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. Old: "raise one leg and swing the lower leg forward and back from the knee ... a second form is a side stretch." New: "Standing, swing one foot forward with a jerk of the knee, then back so that the heel touches the buttock; then the other leg; ten times to begin." Source: Brahmachari reprint, Janu sakti vikasaka. No side stretch form is given. The caution line has no source: mark unverified.
- **Jangha shakti vikasaka, thigh (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. New: "The syllabus lists II-A and II-B. II-A: feet together, arms straight out in front parallel to the floor; bend the knees, keeping them together, until the thighs are level; heels and toes stay down; rise slowly. II-B: heels raised, arms out to the sides; bend with the knees apart without sitting on the heels; rise slowly." Source: Brahmachari reprint, Jangha sakti vikasaka 2; Board syllabus v0.2 unit 4.3a (II-A and B). Jumping jacks are Jangha sakti vikasaka 1 in the reprint, not in the syllabus list: leave out.
- **Pada-mula and gulpha-pada-prishtha-pada-tala, ankle and foot (deck, not yet checked)**. CORRECTED. New: "Pada-mula A: on the toes, rise and lower in a spring-like movement. Pada-mula B: balanced on the toes, jump up and land on the toes. Gulpha-pada-prishtha-pada-tala: hold one foot about nine inches off the ground and circle the ankle each way; then the other foot." Source: Brahmachari reprint, both sections. This settles that B is a jump on the toes; the deck's "bending and stretching of the foot" is not in the text.
