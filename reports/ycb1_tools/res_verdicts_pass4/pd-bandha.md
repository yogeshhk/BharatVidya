## Open items

- **Names and sites: "they guide prana and add stability and focus"** (pass 3 UNVERIFIED). CORRECTED. New: "Traditionally they are said to direct prana: the Hatha Pradipika says uddiyana is so called because prana flies up the central channel by it (3.54 to 3.55), and that jalandhara stops the downward flow (3.70). Take this as tradition, not as a medical claim." Source: Hatha Pradipika chapter 3 (Sinh 1915). Drop "stability and focus" (no text).
- **Uddiyana bandha: empty stomach, full exhalation, release before the inhale** (pass 3 not confirmed). CONFIRMED. APMB, Uddiyana bandha: exhale fully, hold the breath out, chin lock, draw the abdomen in and up; release the lock, raise the head, then inhale slowly; external retention only; 3 rounds, building to 10 over months; done on an empty stomach with empty bowels. Hatha Pradipika 3.56: the belly above the navel pressed back towards the spine.
- **Maha bandha: "the three locks together"** (pass 3 not confirmed). CORRECTED. The syllabus lists only the three bandhas (unit 4.8), so delete the maha bandha bullet. If kept, the Hatha Pradipika form differs from "the three locks together": left heel at the perineum, right foot on the left thigh, fill the breath, chin firm on the chest, mind between the eyebrows, then release slowly and repeat on the other side (Hatha Pradipika 3.19 to 3.21, Sinh 1915).
- **What the Hatha Pradipika says (third chapter): "the three together with the great mudra and the great piercing are called the great bandha"**. CORRECTED. Old: as quoted. New: "Mula bandha: press the perineum with the heel and contract the anus, drawing apana up (3.60). Uddiyana bandha: the belly above the navel is pressed back towards the spine (3.56). Jalandhara bandha: contract the throat and press the chin firmly on the chest (3.69). Maha mudra, maha bandha and maha vedha are three separate practices that the text says work together (3.25). The mudras are to be learnt from a teacher and practised little by little (3.31)." Source: Hatha Pradipika chapter 3, Sinh 1915 (verse numbers as in that translation).
- **Cautions** (pass 3 not confirmed). CORRECTED. New: "Uddiyana: not with colitis, stomach or intestinal ulcer, diaphragmatic hernia, high blood pressure, heart disease, glaucoma or raised intracranial pressure; also not in pregnancy or menstruation. Jalandhara: not with cervical spondylosis, raised intracranial pressure, vertigo, high blood pressure or heart disease. Mula: only under an experienced teacher. Learn all three from a qualified teacher; never force." Source: APMB contra-indications for each bandha; NIOS Uddiyana bandha (pregnancy, menstruation, peptic ulcer).
- **Jalandhara and mula bandha steps**. CONFIRMED. APMB, Jalandhara bandha (inhale, retain, chin pressed to chest, arms straight; release, raise the head, exhale) and Moola bandha (contract and lift the pelvic floor, then hold); Hatha Pradipika 3.60 and 3.69.
