# Pass 4 verdicts: ycb1-pt-lesson-plan (Essentials of a good lesson plan)

Sources used in this file:
- NIOS D.El.Ed, Course 502, Block 2, Unit 8 "Planning Learning Activities", sections on the lesson plan and its characteristics (https://nios.ac.in/media/documents/dled/Block2_502.pdf). Government teacher-education text.
- NIOS, Lesson Plan Format of the Yoga Teacher Training Programme 2020 (https://www.nios.ac.in/media/documents/vocational/dip_naturopathy/Lesson_Plan_Format_of_YTTP_2020.pdf).
- Common Yoga Protocol, Ministry of Ayush, 4th revised edition, May 2019 (copy at https://www.mea.gov.in/images/pdf/common-yoga-protocol-english.pdf), sequence of practices and the note on 45 minutes plus 15 minutes of institutional practices; the 2015 copy in docs has the same order and the General Guidelines.

## Open items

- **Why plan (CORE, not confirmed in pass 3).** CORRECTED.
  - Old: "a lesson plan states the aim, the practices, the order, the time and the cautions, so that the class is safe, balanced and on time."
  - New: "a lesson plan states the aim, the practices, the order, the time and the cautions. A good plan is aimed at a few objectives, brief, flexible and possible to finish in the time available, so that the class is safe, balanced and on time."
  - Source: NIOS D.El.Ed Unit 8, characteristics of a lesson plan (objective-based, comprehensive but brief, flexible, implementable within the time); the NIOS yoga lesson plan format has fields for aim, objectives, duration and "instruction and precaution".
- **Order of a class (CORE, not confirmed as worded).** CORRECTED.
  - Old: "begin with prayer and warm-up, then asanas (standing, sitting, prone, lying), then breathing and pranayama, then meditation, then relaxation and a closing prayer."
  - New: "follow the Board's protocol order: prayer, loosening practices, asanas (standing, sitting, prone, supine, ending in shavasana), kapalabhati, pranayama, dhyana, sankalpa and the closing peace chant (shanti path)."
  - Source: Common Yoga Protocol 2019, sequence of practices (shavasana closes the supine asanas; sankalpa and shanti path end the session); General Guidelines (start with a prayer, end with meditation or silence).
- **Parts of a plan (hand-written, not flagged).** CONFIRMED. NIOS yoga lesson plan format: aim, objectives, teaching aids, introduction of the practice, demonstration, student practice, relaxation, benefits, precautions, time, strength of the class.
- **Planned but flexible (quiz answer).** CONFIRMED. NIOS D.El.Ed Unit 8: "A lesson plan should be flexible", within a plan made in advance.
- **Shape of a plan (deck, not yet checked; already carries a pass 3 source).** CONFIRMED. Same order as the Common Yoga Protocol 2019; the protocol is 45 minutes, and institutions may add 15 minutes of their own practices before the sankalpa.
