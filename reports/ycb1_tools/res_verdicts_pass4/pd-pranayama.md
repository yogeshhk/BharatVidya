## Open items

- **Kapalabhati: one to two minutes a day and the caution list** (pass 3 UNVERIFIED). CORRECTED. Old: "The one to two minutes a day and the blood pressure caution are not stated in the sources read." New: "Common Yoga Protocol: one round is 30 rapid breaths followed by a deep breath; 3 rounds; beginners up to 3 rounds of 20 breaths, increased gradually. Avoid with cardiac conditions, giddiness, high blood pressure, vertigo, chronic nose bleeding, epilepsy, migraine, stroke, hernia or gastric ulcers." Source: Common Yoga Protocol, Kapalabhati, Number of rounds and A word of caution. Drop the minutes measure.
- **Named lists: the Gheranda Samhita eight** (pass 3 UNVERIFIED). CONFIRMED. Gheranda Samhita 5.46 (Vasu 1915): sahita, surya-bheda, ujjayi, sitali, bhastrika, bhramari, murchha and kevali.
- **Named lists: the Hatha Pradipika eight; sahita and kevala as two types**. CONFIRMED. Hatha Pradipika 2.44 (Sinh 1915): surya bhedana, ujjayi, sitkari, sitali, bhastrika, bhramari, murchha, plavini. Hatha Pradipika 2.71: by puraka, rechaka and kumbhaka there are three kinds, but as accompanied or alone there are two, sahita and kevala.
- **Named lists: Patanjali's four kinds** (pass 3 UNVERIFIED). CONFIRMED. Yoga Sutra 2.50 (external, internal and suspended, each regulated by place, time and number, becoming long and subtle) and 2.51 (the fourth, which goes beyond the external and internal spheres). Source: Yoga Sutra with Vyasa and Bhoja (Woods translation), wisdomlib.org.
- **Heating and cooling: bhastrika as heating** (pass 3 UNVERIFIED). REMOVE-OR-MARK-UNVERIFIED. Searched: Hatha Pradipika 2.59 to 2.67 (bhastrika: bellows-like breathing; it "increases the digestive power (the gastric fire)" and removes phlegm, 2.65 to 2.66), Guidebook passages read in pass 3. No text classes bhastrika as a heating pranayama. Drop the quiz answer or reword: "the Hatha Pradipika says bhastrika increases the digestive fire".
- **Open conflict: "Plavini overcomes hunger and thirst"**. CORRECTED. Old (deck FAQ): "Plavini overcomes hunger and thirst." New: "Plavini: the belly is filled with air so that the body floats on water like a lotus leaf (Hatha Pradipika 2.70). The text credits sitali, not plavini, with removing hunger and thirst (2.58)." Both are traditional claims; give them as what the text says. Plavini is outside the Board syllabus list.
- **Open conflict: Hatha Pradipika four limbs (asana, pranayama, mudra, nadanusandhana)**. CONFIRMED as the subject order of its four chapters (Sinh 1915 contents: asana, pranayama, mudras, samadhi with nadanusandhana). Give it as the order of the chapters, which the Hatha Pradipika block covers.
- **Open conflict: 196 sutras versus 195**. Out of this block: the count belongs to the Yoga Sutra block (texts worker). No verdict here.
- **Ujjayi, sheetali timing and the other "(checked: ...)" bullets**. No change: confirmed in pass 3.
