## Open items

- **Meaning (CORE, not confirmed).** CORRECTED.
  - Old: "class management is organising the people, space, time and attention so that the class runs smoothly and safely."
  - New: "class management is planning, guiding and controlling what happens in the class, with its time and space, so that every learner can take part safely and the practice runs smoothly."
  - Source: NIOS D.El.Ed Unit 5 (classroom management needs the ability to plan, control and facilitate interaction suited to the activity, taking account of every learner's needs; time management and space management are named aspects).
- **Need (CORE, not confirmed).** CONFIRMED with a small addition.
  - New wording to use: "it keeps the practice safe, uses the time well, keeps the students calm and interested, and is the first condition of effective teaching."
  - Source: NIOS D.El.Ed Unit 5 ("effective classroom management is the major prerequisite to effective teaching"; a prepared lesson plan reduces wasted time).

  - Old (in the bullet): "story-telling is not named in it."
