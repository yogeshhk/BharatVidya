## Open items

- **Chest breathing: "works the muscles between the ribs"**. CORRECTED. Old: "works the muscles between the ribs". New: "thoracic breathing expands and contracts the rib cage and uses the middle part of the lungs". Source: APMB, Thoracic breathing. The text does not name the intercostal muscles.
- **Purpose: the short natural pause after exhalation** (pass 3 not confirmed). CONFIRMED for yogic breathing: APMB, Yogic breathing ("hold the breath for a few seconds at the end of exhalation; this completes one round"). Say it of yogic breathing only, not of each sectional practice.
- **Rules: exhalation about twice the inhalation** (pass 3 not confirmed). REMOVE-OR-MARK-UNVERIFIED. Searched: APMB sectional and yogic breathing (no ratio; "respiration comfortable and relaxed", "the breath should flow naturally and not be forced"), Common Yoga Protocol (the 1:2 idea is not given for sectional breathing). Remove the ratio; keep "slow, comfortable, never forced".
- **Abdominal and clavicular descriptions; inhalation active, exhalation passive** (pass 3 confirmed against the Guidebook). CONFIRMED with a second source: APMB, Abdominal and Clavicular breathing. Count for yogic breathing: 5 to 10 rounds at first, building to 10 minutes daily (APMB).
