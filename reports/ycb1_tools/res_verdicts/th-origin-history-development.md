In yogic lore Shiva is the first yogi (Adiyogi) and first teacher (Adi Guru), who passed the teaching to the seven sages (saptarishi). Seals and figures of the Indus Saraswati Valley civilisation, dated to about 2700 BC by both the Guidebook and the Protocol, are cited as signs of yoga practice, and the Pashupati figure is named in the Guidebook. - Timeline by period, with names (deck, not yet checked) | "  - **Timeline by period, with names (deck, not yet checked) (checked: Guidebook ch.1 sec 1.2):** the Guidebook groups yoga into four periods: pre-Vedic, Vedic (Vedas and Upanishads), classical (Patanjali's Yoga Sutra) and post-classical (Tantra and hatha yoga). Panini is placed by the Guidebook in the 6th century BC and is listed among the sources of the pre-Vedic to Patanjali phase, not as a Vedic figure. The Gita and the epics belong to the classical stage in the Protocol. The Guidebook names modern masters (Vivekananda's 1893 Chicago address starts the modern spread; Krishnamacharya, Sivananda, Iyengar, Kuvalayananda, Satyananda and others). It lists Swami Yogananda among modern masters but does not name Ramana Maharshi, Adi Shankaracharya, Ramanujacharya, Madhvacharya, Kabir, Tulsidas, Mahavira's five vows or the Buddha's eightfold path as yoga milestones; keep these as general background, not Board content. The Board syllabus asks only for origin, history and development."
- How practices evolved (deck, not yet checked) | "  - **How practices evolved (deck, not yet checked) (checked: Guidebook ch.1 sec 1.2 and 1.5):** early practice was Vedic-ascetic and meditative; Patanjali systematised it into eight limbs; the post-classical yoga masters developed Tantra yoga and then hatha yoga with body-centred practices (cleansing, postures, breath control); modern times added many named styles, including Ashtanga, Iyengar, Power and Vinyasa (the Guidebook names Power, Ashtanga and Vinyasa classes). Kundalini yoga is listed as a traditional school in the Protocol. The nadi and posture sequence on the slide is a summary, not a dated claim."
