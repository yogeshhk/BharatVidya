- Neti (deck, not yet checked) | "  - **Neti (checked: Guidebook ch.2 Shat Kriyas):** cleansing of the nasal passage, learned only from a qualified teacher. Jala neti uses tepid salt water through the nose from a neti pot; sutra neti uses a smooth thread or rubber tube passed through the nose. Kapalabhati is done straight after neti to clear any salt water left in the passage."
- Other cleansing actions known by name (deck, not yet checked) | "  - **Other cleansing actions known by name (checked: Guidebook ch.2 Shat Kriyas):** these are outside the practice list and are learned only from a qualified teacher. Nauli: churning and massage of the abdomen by separating the middle abdominal muscles; the Guidebook gives four forms, madhya (middle), vama (left), dakshina (right) and nauli chalana (rotation). Trataka: steady gazing at a small mark without blinking, traditionally till the eyes water, for the eyes and concentration. Basti: cleansing of the colon, jala basti with water and sthala basti with air. The inner and outer trataka split is common teaching, not in the Guidebook."
