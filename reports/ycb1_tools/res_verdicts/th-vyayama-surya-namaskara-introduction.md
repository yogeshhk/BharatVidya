CORRECTED:
- Sukshma and sthula, short meaning (deck, not yet checked) | "  - **Sukshma and sthula, short meaning (checked: Guidebook ch.7 sec 7.2):** sukshma vyayama is a system of small physical and breathing movements that works the joints one after another from head to toe, warms the body and needs no special preparation or long time; sthula vyayama is the larger, whole-body exercise suited to the gross body. The Guidebook does not state strength and endurance or cardiovascular claims for sthula; keep those as general fitness, not a source fact."
- Surya namaskara, purpose (deck, not yet checked) | "  - **Surya namaskara, purpose (checked: Guidebook ch.8 sec 8.1):** a sequence of twelve postures joined with the breath, offered as salutation to the sun, done in the early morning or evening facing the sun by custom, though the Guidebook says it is not mandatory and may be done at other times on an empty stomach. It loosens the spine and limbs, supports circulation and correct breathing, reduces abdominal fat and prepares the body for asana and pranayama. The Guidebook does not state tone, immunity or readiness for meditation as such; treat those as traditional well-being, not cure."
