## Bullets commented out (with reasons)
- HP Chapter 4: "Techniques for awakening Kundalini". The notes place the rousing of kundalini in the mudra and bandha chapter.
- HP Laya Yoga frame: "Role of Kundalini in the final chapter". Same reason.
- Qualities of a Divine Nature: "Lack of Fickleness (अस्थिरता)". अस्थिरता means instability, the opposite of the quality. The notes give achapala. I did not write a Devanagari replacement because the notes give only the romanised form.

## Titles that may be wrong (left unchanged)
- "Concept of Mental Well-being in Patanjali Yoga (पातञ्जलि योग)", "Yogic Practices in Patanjali Yoga (पातञ्जलि योग)" and the dividers: "पातञ्जलि योग" reads as "Patanjali yoga". "Patanjala Yoga" (पातञ्जल योग) is the usual form and the one the syllabus uses. Suggest "Patanjala Yoga (पातञ्जल योग)".
- "Hatha Yoga Pradipika: Chapter 4 - समाधि Samadhi": the chapter covers nada-anusandhana and samadhi. Suggest "Hatha Yoga Pradipika: Chapter 4 - Nada and Samadhi". The titles also contain " - " hyphens and hard-typed text chapter numbers. These are numbers of the source text (content), so I left them.
- "Qualities of a Divine Nature" is used for two consecutive frames. Suggest adding "(16.1-2)" and "(16.2-3)" if the coverage tool allows.
