## ycb1_theory_intro.tex

### Frames changed
- **Etymology of Yoga**: plain wording. Union is now labelled the devotional and Vedantic reading, and body-mind-spirit the simpler reading. Added the notes line that Patanjali uses yuj mainly as concentration. Goal and means reworded.
- **Definitions of Yoga**: own-words English glosses for YS 1.2, Yoga Vasishtha, Gita 2.48, 2.50 and 6.17, and Katha 2.3.11. The old glosses followed published translations. Devanagari unchanged.
- **Aims/Objectives of Yoga**: five pillars glossed (food, behaviour, conduct, thought, recreation). Aim wording tightened. Added "in public programmes the aim is health and well-being" (notes). The Hindi objective line is kept, with a `%` note that it is the teacher's wording with no named source. A `%` note marks the class objectives as teaching summaries.
- **Misconceptions about Yoga**: each misconception now carries its correction from the notes. Added "replaces medical treatment: no" (notes). Hindi lines kept.
- **Origin of Yoga**: "almost 5,000 years" and Rigveda as first reference are now credited to the Guidebook. Shiva passing the teaching to the seven sages added (notes). English lines added for the Indus seals (debated) and the literature list.
- **Historical Development of Yoga**: added "historians debate" to the Indus seals. Classical period now names the eightfold path. Hatha now "draws on tantric and ascetic traditions". Teacher list trimmed.
- **Key Texts and Influences**: reworded. Kaivalya split into two bullets. Adhibhautika is corrected from "external" to "from other beings" (notes). Pradipika now credited to Svatmarama.
- **Evolution of Yoga Practices**: "medieval period" is replaced by post-classical hatha. Added the eight-limbs line. Kundalini, raja and karma are now called traditional schools, and Iyengar is added to the modern styles (notes).
- **Guiding Principles for Yoga Teachers Practitioners**: reworded. Added a bullet saying the online check, asking before touching, cues and feedback are sound practice, not Board rules.
- **Guiding Principles for Yoga Practitioners**: the attribution line now reads "credited to Swami Vishnudevananda, drawn from Swami Sivananda's teaching".
- **Understanding Key Concepts**: all glosses reworded. Ishvara is corrected to the notes' reading: in Patanjali, a special purusha, not a religious figure. Avidya is tied to the five kleshas. "(maya)" is dropped from prakriti. A `%` note says brahman, maya and upadhi are Advaita terms not defined in the Board sources.
- **Tri-Sharira**: removed "seed of individuality and karma", which the notes call the slide's wording, not the Guidebook's.
- **Five Sheaths**: glosses now follow the notes (outermost, bridge, higher intellect and intuition). "Goal: transcend" is replaced by the notes line on practice working outside in.
- **Pancha Maha Bhuta (property table)**: added a tiny line saying the property lists differ between sources and are not yet checked against the Guidebook. The table itself is unchanged.
- **Sapta Dhatu**: function reworded. Marked as standard Ayurveda; the Guidebook gives the seven for the food sheath.
- **Tri Doshas**: qualities corrected to the Ashtanga Hridaya list in the notes. Dosha now means "that which spoils or goes out of balance"; "cosmic rhythm" removed. Source line added.
- **Pancha Prana**: locations corrected to the notes (Gheranda): heart, anal, throat, navel, whole body. Added a variation line. Upapranas corrected: belching, blinking, hunger and thirst (or sneezing), yawning, and "stays after death".
- **Jnana, Bhakti, Karma, Raja, Hatha, Mantra Yoga**: definitions reworded to the notes. Removed points the notes do not hold:
  - Bhakti: the lover-and-beloved union.
  - Karma: "outgoing nature".
  - Raja: "culmination of all paths".
- **Jnana, Bhakti, Karma, Raja, Hatha, Mantra Yoga** (continued):
  - Gita 7.16 cited for the four devotees.
  - Kirtan's "most important" credited to the Guidebook.
  - Hatha: "sourced from Tantra (Guidebook)", with a clarifier on yama and niyama.
  - Text names corrected to Yoga Ratnavali and Yoga Tarangini (notes).
- **Health: Meaning and Definition**: swasthya reworded; the health list credited to the Guidebook.
- **Strength and Balance in Yoga**: reworded.
- **Joint Mobility and Yogic View of Health**: Shvetashvatara and Vibhuti pada glosses reworded; Yoga Sutra 3.40 added.
- **Yoga Practices for Holistic Well-Being**: pranayama changed from "lung capacity and energy flow" to "traditionally said to calm and lighten". Meditation now "steadies the mind". Personalised practice now "the teacher picks the mix" (notes).
- **Introduction to Shatkarma**: meaning (six actions) and purpose reworded. Added the Pradipika fat-or-phlegm note and the caution that dhauti, basti and nauli are not for beginners (notes).
- **Overview of Shatkarma Techniques**: forms from the notes (jala and sutra neti, jala and sthala basti, nauli churning, trataka without blinking). Added a small line: order varies by school; do kapalabhati after neti.

### Titles possibly wrong (left unchanged)
- `त्रिदोष Tri Doshas: Three Constitutions`: dosha means "that which goes out of balance", not constitution. Suggest `त्रिदोष Tri Doshas: Three Doshas`.
- `Guiding Principles for Yoga Teachers Practitioners`: a word is missing. Suggest `Guiding Principles for Yoga Teachers and Practitioners`.
- `राज  योग  Raja Yoga: The Royal Yoga`: this is fine. The syllabus names "Patanjali" for this school, so the coverage tool may expect "Patanjala".
