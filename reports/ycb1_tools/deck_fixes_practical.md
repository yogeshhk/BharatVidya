Files edited: LaTeX/books/YCB1/workshop/ycb1_practical_demoskills.tex and ycb1_practical_asanas.tex. No frame added or removed; picture lines and their FIG, PROMPT and NEW-PIC comments untouched. Source abbreviations: CYP = Common Yoga Protocol; Guidebook = Board Official Guidebook Level I; Brahmachari = Yogic Suksma Vyayama (reprint); Niranjananand = Pran and Pranayam; Satyananda = Asana Pranayama Mudra Bandha.

## Fixes: demoskills

- Concept of Pranava: A, U, M as creation, preservation, dissolution stated as fact -> marked a popular tradition, not in the Guidebook. Source: pd-prayer verdict.
- Concept of Pranava: "essence of Brahman" -> Guidebook, following Patanjali, names pranava as the word for Ishvara. Source: Guidebook.
- Concept of Pranava: japa as bare repetition; "enhances spiritual awareness", "promotes focus", "deepens connection with the divine" -> japa with meaning in mind; the effects worded as traditionally said. Source: Guidebook, verdict.
- Concept of Hymns: "invokes spiritual energies", "purification" -> devotional practice, devotion and blessings; added that the CYP opens with a Rigveda hymn and closes with sankalpa and shanti path. Source: CYP.
- Dhauti: "removing toxins", "digestive health and detoxification" -> qualified teacher, cleansing up to the stomach, not a cure; danda dhauti tube (Gheranda Samhita plant stalk); vastra moistened strip drawn out under a guru. Source: Guidebook.
- Neti: sutra neti "rubber thread, massages and opens blockages" -> smooth thread or rubber tube; kapalabhati follows neti; qualified teacher; jala neti tepid salt water. Source: Guidebook.
- Kapalabhati (cleansing): "cleanses frontal lobes, improves brain function", "expels impurities as CO2" -> frontal region of head and lungs (CYP: frontal air sinuses); CYP rounds. Calls it a kriya many treat as pranayama. Source: Guidebook, CYP.
- Nauli: organ-purifying list and "improves digestion and appetite" -> churning and massage of the abdomen; four forms including nauli chalana. Source: Guidebook.
- Trataka: "objects that give pleasure" -> candle flame or small mark; epilepsy caution; antar and bahir split labelled common teaching, not Guidebook. Source: Guidebook, Satyananda.
- Basti: "cures 50 percent of abdominal diseases", both types draw water, "colon nourishes almost all tissues" -> cure claim removed; jala basti water, sthala basti air; qualified teacher. Source: Guidebook.
- Sukshma Vyayama Concept: wrist movements and toe touches as examples -> the syllabus joint list. Source: Board syllabus.
- Neck Movement: "enhances flexibility and strength" -> two rounds, slow, with breath; cautions; protocol order noted. Source: CYP.
- Shoulder Movement: arm circles and shrugs, rotations, arm raises -> Brahmachari forms (raise sideways overhead; fist circles). Benefit softened. Source: Brahmachari.
- Trunk Movement: first, second forms matched to Brahmachari; fourth form "side twists" -> side bends; fifth -> semicircle swing; "fast" bends -> begin gently, no hand contact; "strengthens core" -> loosens spine and waist; CYP cautions. Source: Brahmachari, CYP.
- Knee Movement: "side stretches" -> removed; heel to buttock swing described. Source: Brahmachari, CYP.
- Thighs Movement: first practice mislabeled "II-A" -> labeled I and noted that the Board names only II; II-A and II-B steps corrected. Source: Brahmachari, Board syllabus.
- Ankle Movement: steps tightened; benefits softened; jump on toes kept (Brahmachari confirms). Source: Brahmachari.
- Sthula Vyayama Concept: strength and endurance aims and the push-up, squat, plank, lunge, leg raise list -> two syllabus practices; aims as traditionally credited. Source: Board syllabus, verdict.
- Sarvanga Pushti: general strength lines -> steps from Brahmachari; "contradictions" typo -> cautions, general precaution only. Source: Brahmachari.
- Hrid Gati: "cardio exercise" lines -> steps and breath from Brahmachari. Source: Brahmachari.
- Comparison of Sukshma and Sthula: strength and endurance wording -> gross body, whole-body movements. Source: verdict.
- Surya Namaskara Concept: energize and purify, digestion, calm focus, weight management, detox, immune system -> removed or marked traditional, not cures; custom not mandatory; fuller cautions; breath not forced. Source: Guidebook.
- Sequence of Surya Namaskara: fifth and eighth postures "plank" and "downward dog" -> parvatasana; eight-limb salute "retain" -> breathe normally, no forceful retention. Source: Guidebook.
- Sequence (mantra frame): single mantra "Om Shri Savitri Surya Narayanaya Namah" -> the twelve names said silently, mantras optional. Source: Guidebook.
- Preparatory Breathing Overview: lung capacity, "integrates with asanas" -> oxygen intake without effort; three sections join into the full yogic breath. Source: Guidebook.
- Abdominal Breathing: "exhale through the mouth, contracting abdomen" -> nose, passive exhale, abdomen flattens; oxygenation claim -> chest still, do not draw in on inhale. Source: Guidebook, CYP.
- Chest Breathing: "intercostal muscles" and "increases lung capacity" -> rib cage widens; less air, dominant under stress. Source: Guidebook.
- Clavicular Breathing: "expanding lung capacity" -> shallow, last step before full breath. Source: Guidebook.
- Combination Breathing: exhale in reverse as one smooth wave. Source: Guidebook.
- Pranayama Concept: "control of breath", essential for health, regulates energy flow -> regulating and lengthening; fourth limb; with or without kumbhaka. Source: Guidebook, CYP.
- Types of Pranayama and Sitali frame title: "Sitali" -> "Sheetali" (spelling). Source: verdict.
- Benefits of Pranayama: lung capacity, nervous system, detoxification -> calm, breath-mind link, lower stress, not medical claims. Source: Guidebook, CYP.
- Anulom Vilom: hemispheres claim and 5 to 10 minutes -> nasagra mudra sequence, five rounds, ratio. Source: CYP.
- Kapalabhati (pranayama frame): "increases lung capacity and alertness", 1 to 2 minutes -> CYP rounds and full caution list. Source: CYP.
- Bhramari: minutes -> five rounds; mood claims removed; shanmukhi mudra, ear and nose infection caution. Source: CYP.
- Ujjayi: concentration, energy, nervous system claims -> sitting, standing or lying; partly closed throat. Source: Guidebook.
- Sheetali: pursed lips, hot weather -> rolled tongue (sheetkari teeth); cautions. Source: Guidebook.
- Mudra Overview: "channel energy", "energy leaves via fingers" -> marked tradition. Source: verdict.
- Types of Mudras: bandhas as internal locks under mudra -> school grouping, listed separately by the Board. Source: Board syllabus.
- Hasta Mudras: jnana palms down -> palms up per CYP (variants noted); chin "palms up, chest opens" -> same contact as jnana, confirm Board form; chinmaya "humbleness" -> manifest consciousness, fingers folded in; brahma "good for overall health" -> removed, form from Bihar School; adi "stores energy" -> removed; nasika matched to nasagra mudra. Source: CYP, Niranjananand.
- Kaya Mudras: inverted postures as kaya mudras -> labeled a school grouping, not in Board syllabus, items marked as asanas with neck and blood pressure caution. Source: verdict.
- Mukh Mudras: "enhances mental focus and inner vision" -> traditional wording. Source: verdict.
- Bandhas: "regulates energy", "enhances stability and focus" -> added one-line descriptions (Hatha Pradipika, Satyananda) and an uddiyana and maha bandha caution line. Source: Hatha Pradipika, Satyananda, NIOS.
- Chakra Mudras: "aligns energy centers", "supports spiritual growth" -> tradition wording, forms vary. Source: verdict.
- Practices Leading to Meditation: added line that the list is a teaching sequence, not the Board order. Source: Board syllabus.

- Padahastasana: "calms the mind" removed; cautions list. Source: CYP.
- Kati Chakrasana: "massages abdominal organs, improves digestion" removed (no source).

- Vajrasana: "aids digestion, relieves back pain" -> removed; knee osteoarthritis caution. Source: CYP.
- Bhadrasana: cushion step; severe arthritis and sciatica. Source: CYP.
- Ushtrasana: cautions list. Source: CYP, Guidebook.
- Shashankasana: steps and cautions. Source: CYP.
- Vakrasana: foot beside knee, arm around knee; massage claim removed; cautions. Source: CYP.
- Bhujangasana: lift chin and chest to the navel region; stress management; cautions. Source: CYP.
- Shalabhasana: legs only, chin on floor, palms up; cautions. Source: CYP.

- Pavanamuktasana: head-lift step and benefits as in CYP.
- Uttanapadasana / Ardha Halasana: 45 degrees removed; note that the Board lists two separate postures. Source: Board syllabus, verdict.
- Setubandhasana: hold ankles; ulcer, hernia, pregnancy cautions. Source: CYP.
