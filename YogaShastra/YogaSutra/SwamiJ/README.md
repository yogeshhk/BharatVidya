# Swami J: Yoga Sutras study material

Notes, outlines and study aids on Patanjali's Yoga Sutras from Swami J's site, which the Yoga Sutra slides in this
repository credit (see their introduction and references frames). Copies of the PDFs were once kept here. They are not in this
repository any more, because they belong to their author. Read them at the source:

- Site home: https://www.swamij.com/
- The Yoga Sutras pages: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras.htm
  - Introduction: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-intro.htm
  - About Patanjali: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-patanjali.htm
  - Narrative style: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-narrative.htm
  - Reminder questions and answers: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-questions.htm
  - Seven keys to practice: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-keys.htm
- Coordinating the four functions of mind: https://www.swamij.com/fourfunctionsmind.htm
- A plain-text Yoga Sutras page that one of the saved files came from: http://www.rainbowbody.net/HeartMind/Yogasut_plain.htm

The folder also held *The Science of Yoga* by I. K. Taimni, which is a published book: borrow or buy it. The two
pictures the decks use (`SwamiJ_LearningPath.png`, `SwamiJ_YogasutraCard.png`) have their own copies in `LaTeX/images/`.

See [NOTICE.md](../../../NOTICE.md) for how this repository treats third-party material. If you are the author and
want something changed, open an issue.
