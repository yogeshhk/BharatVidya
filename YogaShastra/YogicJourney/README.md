# Yogic Journey

A series of daily posts, "30 Days of Yogic Journey", written by Saatvik Life on Medium: an introduction to yoga, the
guiding principles for practitioners, the yogic cleansing practices (Shat Karma), Surya Namaskara, the Sukshma
(subtle) and Sthula (gross) Vyayama exercise sets, yogic diet, Yoga Nidra and the classification of asanas.

Copies of the posts were once kept here as PDFs. They are not in this repository any more, because they belong to
their author. Read them at the source:

- Author's Medium page: https://saatviklife.medium.com/ (each post is titled "Day NN of 30 Days of Yogic Journey" followed by its topic)

See [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md) for how this repository treats third-party material. If you are the author and want
something changed, open an issue.
