## Third-party material

- Slides carry a source line (`Ref: ...`) naming where a point comes from. The interpretations tagged with initials on the Yoga Sutra slides come from the Yoga Sutra Study site; the Sanskrit analysis and meanings come from patanjaliyogasutra.in.
- A copy of the YogaSutraTrees project of Giacomo De Luca used to be kept here. It has been removed: it is his project and keeps its own licence and credit. Use his repository; the links are in `YogaSutra/SOURCES.md`.
- The Orientation material draws on a lecture by Miska Käppi, "I Was Told Patanjali Founded Yoga. Then I Read His Book." The lecturer's own views are marked as such in the notes.
- Books, recordings and course packs that were kept in earlier versions of this repository have been removed from it. Where they were useful, link to the publisher, library or the author's own page instead.

If you are an author or rights holder and want something changed or removed, open an issue and it will be handled promptly.

## What was removed, and why

This repository used to hold a large pile of study material. On 2026-11-14 it was cut down to what is original and complete enough to be useful to a stranger. The rest was moved out and is kept privately by the maintainer.

Moved out (kept privately, not published):
- **Personal coursework:** study sheets, answers, evaluations, notes and certificates from courses the maintainer took (a Dasbodh by-email course, Samskrita Bharati courses, a master's course in Chanakya Kautilya Studies, a yoga teacher-training course).
- **Third-party books, course packs and recordings:** published books and scans, course handouts, audio and course material from other people and institutions. They belong to their authors and publishers.
- **Saved web pages, papers and magazine volumes:** printed or saved copies. Where the source is public it is linked instead: see [YogaSutra/SOURCES.md](YogaSutra/SOURCES.md) and the two reading lists below.
- **Public official documents** (for example UGC, AICTE and IIT outlines on Indian Knowledge Systems, Yoga Certification Board syllabi): public already, so find them at their official sites.
- **Other people's projects:** the copy of the YogaSutraTrees knowledge graph. Use the original: link in [YogaSutra/SOURCES.md](YogaSutra/SOURCES.md).
- **Auto-generated starter notebooks** and working backups.

Removed because unfinished: four slide decks were deleted rather than kept half-done: an Arthashastra workshop (a stub), a Bharat Vidya overview (unsourced), a Zen Yoga workshop (a book summary) and a Shrimath seminar (a summary of a teacher's course).

Reading lists that stay:
- [Sanskrit and computing: papers and articles](Samskrit/Reading_list_papers.md)
- [Yoga Nidra: research and sleep](YogaNidra/Reading_list_research.md)
