# Yoga Sutra: sources behind the slides and notes

The slides and the notes draw on the sources below. They are linked, not hosted here: copies belong to their authors. For
a longer list of videos and lectures see [README.md](README.md).

## Swami J
Notes, outlines and study aids on the Yoga Sutras. The slides credit this site in their introduction and references.
- Site: https://www.swamij.com/
- The Yoga Sutras pages: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras.htm
  - Introduction: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-intro.htm
  - About Patanjali: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-patanjali.htm
  - Narrative style: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-narrative.htm
  - Reminder questions and answers: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-questions.htm
  - Seven keys to practice: https://swamij.com/yoga-sutras-keys.htm
- Coordinating the four functions of mind: https://www.swamij.com/fourfunctionsmind.htm

## Yoga Sutra Study
The slides use its interpretations, tagged with initials.
- Site: http://yogasutrastudy.info/
- Audio recordings of the sutras: http://yogasutrastudy.info/links/ysp-multi-track.html
- About the maintainer: http://yogawithsubhash.com/about/

[`YogSutra_Devnagari.txt`](YogSutra_Devnagari.txt) in this folder is the Devanagari text of the sutras. If you reuse it, check the source site first for how it should be credited.

## YogaSutraTrees
A machine-readable knowledge graph of the Yoga Sutras by Giacomo De Luca. A copy used to sit in this repository; it is his project, so use his:
- Repository: https://github.com/Giacomo-De-Luca/YogaSutraTrees
- Website (beta): https://giacomo-de-luca.github.io/YogaSutraTrees/
- Gitbook: https://project-patanjali.gitbook.io/yoga-sutra-trees/
