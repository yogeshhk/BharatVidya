# Yogasutra

What is in this folder: the study notes in [Notes on Yogasutra](Notes%20on%20Yogasutra.md); the Devanagari text ([`YogSutra_Devnagari.txt`](refs/YogSutra_Devnagari.txt) and its [headings](refs/YogSutra_Devnagari_headings.txt)); hand-drawn [mind maps](mindmaps/) of the four pada, as PDF and SVG; [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md), the sources behind the slides and notes; the notes as PDF ([`Notes_PDF/`](refs/)); and two public texts in [`Texts/`](refs/): the [Sanskrit sutras only](refs/YogaSutras_only_Sanskrit.pdf) and [Vivekananda's Patanjali in Sanskrit and English](refs/Patanjali_YogaSutra_Vivekananda_Sanskrit_English.pdf) (Vivekananda's text is in the public domain; the source of this particular scan was not recorded). A [sketchnote on the Samadhi pada](mindmaps/Yogasutra_Samadhi_Sketchnote.jpg) sits with the mind maps. The slides themselves are in [`../LaTeX/`](../LaTeX/README.md). Below is a long list of video lectures and other resources.
