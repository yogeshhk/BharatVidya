# What was removed from this repository, and why

This repository used to hold a large pile of study material. On 2026-11-14 it was cut down to what is original and
complete enough to be useful to a stranger. The rest was moved out and is kept privately by the maintainer.

## Moved out (kept privately, not published)
- **Personal coursework:** study sheets, answers, evaluations, notes and certificates from courses the maintainer took (a Dasbodh by-email course, Samskrita Bharati courses, a master's course in Chanakya Kautilya Studies, a yoga teacher-training course).
- **Third-party books, course packs and recordings:** published books and scans, course handouts, audio and course material from other people and institutions. They belong to their authors and publishers.
- **Saved web pages, papers and magazine volumes:** printed or saved copies. Where the source is public it is linked instead: see [YogaSutra/SOURCES.md](YogaSutra/SOURCES.md) and the two reading lists below.
- **Public official documents** (for example UGC, AICTE and IIT outlines on Indian Knowledge Systems, Yoga Certification Board syllabi): public already, so find them at their official sites.
- **Other people's projects:** the copy of the YogaSutraTrees knowledge graph. Use the original: link in [YogaSutra/SOURCES.md](YogaSutra/SOURCES.md).
- **Auto-generated starter notebooks** and working backups.

## Removed because unfinished
Four slide decks were deleted rather than kept half-done: an Arthashastra workshop (a stub), a Bharat Vidya overview
(unsourced), a Zen Yoga workshop (a book summary) and a Shrimath seminar (a summary of a teacher's course).
