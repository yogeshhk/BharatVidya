# Contents

A map of what is in this repository. See the [root README](./README.md) for its purpose and background, and
[MOVED_MATERIAL.md](./MOVED_MATERIAL.md) for what was removed and why. Each theme has its own folder; the slides of
all themes are together in `LaTeX/`.

## Yoga Sutra (`YogaSutra/`)
- [README](./YogaSutra/README.md): the folder guide and a long list of video lectures
- [Notes on Yogasutra](./YogaSutra/Notes%20on%20Yogasutra.md): the study notes
- `YogSutra_Devnagari.txt` and `YogSutra_Devnagari_headings.txt`: the Devanagari text and its headings
- `mindmaps/`: hand-drawn mind maps of the four pada, each as PDF and SVG
- [SOURCES.md](./YogaSutra/SOURCES.md): links to the sources behind the slides and notes, including the YogaSutraTrees knowledge graph

## Yoga Nidra (`YogaNidra/`)
- [README](./YogaNidra/README.md): an overview of Yoga Nidra with references
- [Notes](./YogaNidra/Notes_YogaNidra.md) and a [detailed practice script](./YogaNidra/YogaNidraDetailedScript.md)
- [Research reading list](./YogaNidra/Reading_list_research.md)

## Yoga (`Yoga/`)
- [README](./Yoga/README.md): a curated list of Yoga resources
- [Meditation notes](./Yoga/Notes_Meditation.md)

## Samskrit (`Samskrit/`)
- [Awesome Samskrit](./Samskrit/Awesome_samskrit.md): Sanskrit learning resources
- [Awesome Arthashastra](./Samskrit/Awesome_arthashastra.md): Kautilya's Arthashastra
- [Sanskrit and computing: papers and articles](./Samskrit/Reading_list_papers.md): a reading list

## Slides and cheat sheets (`LaTeX/`)
- [LaTeX build system and file guide](./LaTeX/README.md): the Yoga Sutra workshop and seminars, the Yoga Shastra and YCB1 workshops, the Yoga Nidra seminar, the Sharada script seminar and the one-pagers, with their topic files, images and build scripts
