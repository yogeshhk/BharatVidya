\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Field Experience: Marks and Format}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Marks}: 10 of the 140 practical marks.
\item \textbf{Where it is assessed}: inside the practical assessment, which lasts at most one hour per candidate.
\item \textbf{What it asks}: a report of a training programme you conducted, with photographs, a feedback form filled by a trainee, and your experience details in the Board's format.
\item \textbf{Preparation}: keep a short log of the classes you have taken (place, group, what you taught, hours, feedback) in a park, a society or a workplace, and be ready to talk about one of them.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny (Ref: YCB, Guidelines for Yoga Professionals, Version 0.3, Annexures A and B; the log is a study suggestion, not an official requirement)}
\end{frame}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{The Board's Two Forms}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Feedback form (Annexure A)}: filled by a trainee: name and contact details, period and days of training, mode of training (home based, park, society, corporate, other), reason for taking up yoga, feedback about the trainer, and a mark out of 10; signed and dated.
\item \textbf{Experience format (Annexure B)}: persons trained year by year with number and mode; working experience with an organisation; yoga camps held; totals of persons, hours and camps.
\item Signed by the candidate; a candidate from an institute also needs the institute's authorised signatory. An open candidate needs no institute signature.
\item No exemption at this level: it is only for postgraduate yoga degree holders sitting the Yoga Master certification.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny (Use the Board's current format; the layout may change with a new version.)}
\end{frame}
