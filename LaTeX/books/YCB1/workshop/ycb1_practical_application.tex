\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Application of Knowledge: Marks and Format}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Marks}: 10 of the 140 practical marks.
\item \textbf{Where it is assessed}: inside the practical assessment, which lasts at most one hour per candidate.
\item \textbf{What it asks}: the Board names this part and its marks, but does not describe it. The points below are study ideas only.
\item \textbf{Preparation}: work out a few cases, such as a beginner, an older adult and a person with a stiff back. Say what you would keep, change or leave out.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny (Ref: YCB, Guidelines for Yoga Professionals, Version 0.3; the case examples are study suggestions, not official questions)}
\end{frame}
