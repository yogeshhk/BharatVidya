\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{How the Exam Works}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Two stages}: theory and practical; both are compulsory, and absence in either counts as a fail.
\item \textbf{Theory}: 60 one-mark multiple-choice questions in two hours, spread equally over the three units; OMR sheet; no negative marking.
\item \textbf{Language}: the paper is in English and Hindi; where they differ, the English version holds.
\item \textbf{Practical}: at most one hour per candidate, length set by the assessor; covers demonstration, teaching, application of knowledge and field experience.
\item \textbf{Passing}: at least 70\% in theory and at least 70\% in practical, each on its own; up to 5\% grace marks in one of the two stages; a decimal result is rounded up.
\item \textbf{If you fail one stage}: pass practical but fail theory, and you may reappear for theory once within 3 months; pass theory but fail practical, and you appear again as a fresh candidate.
\item \textbf{Certificate}: valid for 5 years; renew through the Continuing Yoga Education Programme.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny (Ref: YCB, Guidelines for Yoga Professionals, Version 0.3; check the current YCB notice before an exam)}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Who Can Sit, and Pass Marks}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The level}: first level of the Board's scheme, the Yoga Protocol Instructor; teaches the basics of the common yoga protocol, for example in parks and housing societies.
\item \textbf{Who can sit}: open candidates need no qualification; there is no age limit.
\item \textbf{Course entry}: secondary school is suggested, but an institution may set its own rule.
\item \textbf{Training time}: not less than 200 hours, or 3 months part time, or 1 month full time; 12 credit points.
\item \textbf{Pass marks}: theory 70\% of 60 is 42; practical 70\% of 140 is 98. A high score in one stage does not make up for a low score in the other.
\item \textbf{Absence}: absence in either stage counts as a fail; in an exceptional case and on merit, the institution may allow a candidate who sat the theory to take the practical on another date.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{After the Result}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Practical passed, theory failed}: sit the theory once more within 3 months of the result, paying the prescribed fee.
\item \textbf{Theory passed, practical failed}: counts as failed; apply again as a fresh candidate, paying the full assessment fee but not a new enrolment fee; the enrolment number stays valid.
\item \textbf{Re-checking}: within 15 days of the result, for a fee, in your presence; the agency replies within 30 days.
\item \textbf{What is re-checked}: only the arithmetic of the theory sheet; no copy of the paper or sheet may be kept.
\item \textbf{Certificate}: unique number and security code; valid 5 years from the day the result is declared; renew through the Continuing Yoga Education Programme before it expires.
\item \textbf{Fees}: enrolment fee paid once for a type of certification, valid for life; exam fee set by the institution; separate fees for re-checking and a duplicate; read current amounts from the Board.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{On the Exam Day}
\begin{itemize}
\item The admit card is issued at least a week before; download it from the Board's site.
\item Carry it with a valid identity card and a pen.
\item Reach the centre preferably an hour early; a candidate more than 15 minutes late is not allowed in.
\item Results are declared within 30 days.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Quick check}
\begin{enumerate}
\item What is the pass mark in the theory stage?
\item A candidate passes the practical and fails the theory. What can the candidate do?
\item Is there negative marking in the theory paper?
\item How long is a Level 1 certificate valid, and how is it renewed?
\end{enumerate}
{\small Answers: 70\% of the theory marks, separate from the practical; sit the theory once more within 3 months of the result; no; five years, renewed through the Continuing Yoga Education Programme before it expires.}
