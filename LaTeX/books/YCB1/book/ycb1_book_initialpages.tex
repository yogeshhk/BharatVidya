\chapter*{Preface}

This is a small, plain guide for the Level 1 exam of the Yoga Certification Board, Yoga Protocol Instructor.
It is complete to the Board's syllabus and does not go outside it. It is short on purpose: the official
guidebook is long and formal, and a candidate needs something to revise from.

\chapter*{How to Use This Book}

Read a chapter, then answer its quiz without looking. Practise the demonstration items with a teacher. Use
the exam chapter to plan your preparation against the marks.
