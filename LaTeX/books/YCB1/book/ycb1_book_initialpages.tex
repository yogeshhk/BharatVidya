\chapter*{Important Notice}
\thispagestyle{plain}

\textbf{This book is for education and exam preparation. It is not medical advice.}

\begin{itemize}
\item The book does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Yoga supports well-being; it does not replace medical care. Where a chapter speaks of benefits, they are effects the tradition credits, not medical claims.
\item Yoga practice carries physical risk. Postures, breathing practices, locks and cleansing actions can cause harm if done wrongly or by a person for whom they are not suitable. Learn every practice from a qualified, certified teacher. Never learn the cleansing actions, the locks or forceful breathing from a book.
\item Consult a physician before starting if you have a heart condition, high or low blood pressure, a back or neck problem, a hernia, an ulcer, asthma, epilepsy, an injury, a recent surgery, or if you are pregnant. Stop at once if you feel pain, dizziness or breathlessness. Read the cautions given with each practice.
\item The chapter on first aid and CPR is a summary for the syllabus and does not replace a hands-on course with a trained instructor. In an emergency, call the emergency services first.
\item Health, first-aid and Ayurvedic content has not yet been reviewed by a qualified reviewer, and the practices have not yet been reviewed by a certified teacher. Chapters marked ``Not yet fact-checked'' are drafts.
\item Exam rules, marks and dates are those of the Yoga Certification Board at the time of writing and may change; check the Board's current documents before you apply.
\item The author and publisher accept no liability for any injury, loss or damage arising from the use of this book.
\end{itemize}

\chapter*{Preface}

This is a small, plain guide for the Level 1 exam of the Yoga Certification Board, Yoga Protocol Instructor.
It is complete to the Board's syllabus and does not go outside it. It is short on purpose: the official
guidebook is long and formal, and a candidate needs something to revise from.

\chapter*{How to Use This Book}

Read a chapter, then answer its quiz without looking. Practise the demonstration items with a teacher. Use
the exam chapter to plan your preparation against the marks.
