# Book Proposal: Yoga Certification Board Level 1 Exam Guide

*For the Yoga Protocol Instructor*

**Yogesh Haribhau Kulkarni**

## Overview

Candidates for the Level 1 exam of the Yoga Certification Board (Yoga Protocol Instructor, Ministry of AYUSH) study from an official guidebook that is long, formal and hard to revise from, and from scattered notes and videos. What they lack is a short, plain book that covers exactly what the exam tests.

This book is that guide. It follows the Board's current Level 1 syllabus and guideline topic by topic, covers every topic in them and nothing outside them, and states the source of its facts. It runs from the meaning, aim and history of yoga, its principles, schools and texts, through the body, health, daily routine and first aid, to the practices the exam asks a candidate to demonstrate: cleansing techniques, vyayama, Surya Namaskara, the syllabus asanas, pranayama, bandha, mudra and meditation. It ends with teaching a class, planning lessons, and the exam itself: its parts, its marks and the certificate.

Each chapter is written for revision: plain explanations, the steps and cautions of each practice, and a quiz to answer without looking. Every fact is being checked against a named source; until its check is done, a chapter is marked as not yet fact-checked.

## What Makes This Book Different

- **Complete to the syllabus, nothing outside it.** The Board's syllabus is the spine; every unit and topic has its chapter or section.
- **Written to revise from.** Short, plain and structured for the exam, in place of a long formal guidebook.
- **Sourced and fact-checked.** Names, periods, Sanskrit texts, practice steps, cautions, health statements and exam rules are checked against named sources; the health and first-aid content will be reviewed by a qualified person before print.
- **Practical and theory together.** The demonstration items (practices, teaching a class, lesson planning) sit beside the theory the written exam tests.
- **Exam-focused.** A chapter on the exam, its components and marks, and a quiz in every chapter.
- **Part of a teaching kit.** The book pairs with a Level 1 workshop deck and cheat sheet.

## Audience

- **Primary:** candidates preparing for the Yoga Certification Board Level 1 exam (Yoga Protocol Instructor).
- **Secondary:** yoga teachers and training institutes who prepare candidates; practitioners who want a structured, sourced overview of the Level 1 syllabus.
- **Prerequisites:** none. No earlier study of yoga texts or anatomy is assumed; practical items should be learnt with a teacher.

The first two are named from the copies the author holds; the last two are named from current retail listings (Amazon India), described as their listings describe them.

| Title | Position | How this book differs |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Yoga Certification Board, *Certification of Yoga Professionals: Official Guidebook for Level I (Instructor)*, first edition (New Delhi, 2016) | Official, long and formal (about 225 pages); written for the older four-level scheme | Short and plain; follows the Board's current Level 1 syllabus exactly |

| Satpal Khichar, *Yoga Question Bank: Yoga Certification & UGC-NET Yoga*; *Yoga MCQ Series for YCB (Level 1, 2, 3) & UGC-NET/JRF Yoga* (Notion Press, 2020) | Question practice across all YCB levels and the UGC-NET yoga paper | Teaches each Level 1 topic before testing it, with sources, and carries a quiz per chapter |

## Contents

**Front matter:** Important Notice; Preface; How to Use This Book.

**Foundations.** Yoga: meaning, aim and history; guiding principles for practitioners; principles and schools of yoga; the yoga texts; Hatha texts, diet and healthy living.

**Body and health.** Yogic practices for health; the human body; health promotion and the yogic view of the person; daily routine, stress and prevention; first aid and CPR.

**Practices.** Prayer, pranava and hymns; cleansing techniques (dhauti, neti and kapalabhati); sukshma and sthula vyayama; Surya Namaskara; the syllabus asanas; breathing practices and pranayama; bandha and mudra; practices leading to meditation.

**Teaching and the exam.** Teaching a yoga class; planning and conducting lessons; application of knowledge; field experience; the exam, the marks and the certificate.

**Back matter:** References and Further Reading.
