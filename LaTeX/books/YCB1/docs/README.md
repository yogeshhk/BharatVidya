## Yoga Certification Board: official papers

Public documents of the Yoga Certification Board (YCB, Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India), kept here as a convenient
local copy for people preparing for the certification levels. They are not the author's work. The Board owns them.
The current versions are always on the Board's own site: [yogacertificationboard.nic.in](https://yogacertificationboard.nic.in/).
Check there before relying on a syllabus, because the Board revises them.
