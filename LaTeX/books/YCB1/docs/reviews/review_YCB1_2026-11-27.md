## Recomputation ledger (Pass C)

- Exam marks: theory 60 over three equal units gives 20 per unit. Practical 80 + 40 + 10 + 10 = 140. Total
  60 + 140 = 200. Correct.
- Pass marks at 70 percent: 0.7 x 60 = 42; 0.7 x 140 = 98. With up to 5 percent grace in one stage: 3 marks in
  theory or 7 in practical. Consistent with the notes' rule (decimal results rounded up).
- Gita 16.1 to 16.3: verse 1 names nine qualities, verse 2 names eleven, verse 3 names six: 26. The notes' first
  group (the nine of verse 1 plus ahimsa, satya, akrodha, tyaga, shanti) is 14; the second (the remaining six of
  verse 2 plus the six of verse 3) is 12; 14 + 12 = 26. Correct.
- Kapalabhati (Common Yoga Protocol): 3 rounds of 30 breaths = 90 strokes; beginners 3 x 20 = 60. Consistent.
- CPR: at 100 to 120 a minute, a set of 30 compressions takes 15 to 18 seconds. Consistent with "swap every two
  minutes" (about six or seven cycles).
