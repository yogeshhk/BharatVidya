\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{How the Exam Works}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Two stages}: Theory and Practical; both must be taken, and absence in either counts as a fail.
\item \textbf{Theory}: 60 multiple-choice questions of 1 mark each, answered on an OMR sheet, no negative marking, questions in English and Hindi.
\item \textbf{Practical}: each candidate is assessed for at most one hour (demonstration, teaching skill, application of knowledge and field experience).
\item \textbf{Passing}: 70\% in theory and 70\% in practical, separately; up to 5\% grace marks in one of them.
\item \textbf{If you fail one stage}: pass practical but fail theory, and you may reappear for theory once within 3 months; pass theory but fail practical, and you appear again as a fresh candidate.
\item \textbf{Certificate}: valid for 5 years; renewal needs the Continuing Yoga Education Program.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny (Ref: YCB, Guidelines for Yoga Professionals, Version 0.2; check the current YCB notice before an exam)}
